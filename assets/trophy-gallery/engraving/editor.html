P2K 1WL Engraving POC v1.9

Unified POC for medals, pre-2026 cups, and 2026 crystal trophies. Trophy lettering now uses the same full-SANS engraved italic engraving language as the approved medal lettering. Medal rendering now uses the approved long-ribbon transparent blanks, the approved inner-circle text zones, and the full-SANS engraved italic proportions derived from the 2025 1WL medal references. Crystal trophies now reuse the same embedded full-SANS engraving settings as the medals/cups, and the bronze/silver top safe zone is trimmed on the left to stay off the metal.
