Stored-rating recruitment analysis
Top-player strategy. The assistant uses the match's Classical or Chess960 rating category automatically. It reads current P2K members from the stored rating snapshot, excludes already registered and unrated members, applies the match rating range and the opponent's lowest registered rating as a floor, excludes members of the opponent club, then tests the strongest remaining players first until a significant lineup advantage is reached.
