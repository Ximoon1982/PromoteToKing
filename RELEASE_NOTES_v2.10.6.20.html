GFFL terminal-debt corrective

This release fixes repeated Green Factorized Freshness Lane requests for matches whose current Chess.com detail resource is terminal, including a Daily team match legitimately cancelled while still in registration and subsequently returning 404.

Existing stranded terminal/ineligible debt self-heals when GFFL is planned or observed. Trusted historical Green facts are preserved.

No schema migration, database reset/reseed, CRON edit, routing change, scoring change, heatmap formula change, or artwork change is required.
