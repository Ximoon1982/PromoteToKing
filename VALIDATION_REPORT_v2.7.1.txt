Environment limitation
----------------------
No production database credentials or live production MariaDB service were available in the build environment. Therefore the destructive Apply action was not executed against production. Its SQL, authentication, locking, validation and transaction paths were statically reviewed and syntax-tested. Use the validation-only command, a full production backup and the documented paused-task procedure before the real Apply.
